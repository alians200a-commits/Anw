- Semantic accuracy wins over keeping every icon in one pack.

- Anesthesia equipment should prefer anesthesia machine, laryngoscope, airway equipment, or patient monitoring; avoid generic stethoscope/scalpel when a precise animated asset exists.
- IV fluids must use an IV bag/infusion visual.
- No infinite animation loops in the app.
- Play motion on entry/press/open only and keep it short.
- Prefer local licensed Lottie/GIF assets over runtime hotlinks.
- Keep a semantically acceptable animated fallback until the licensed IconScout asset is imported.
- Keep Header / Footer / Logo unchanged.
- Do not change medical copy while performing icon work.
